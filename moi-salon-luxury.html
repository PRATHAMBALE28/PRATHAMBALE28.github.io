Notes From The Styling Floor
Colour

Foils, Not Filters

Balayage and foils are having a moment again — but the ask has changed. Fewer clients want a dramatic transformation; more want colour that grows out soft instead of leaving a hard line at the roots.

The shift is toward fewer, better-placed foils rather than a full head of colour — it costs less to maintain and still looks intentional eight weeks in. If your last colour left a visible regrowth line within a month, that's usually a placement problem, not a hair problem.

— Notes from the Moi styling floor
Men's Cuts

The Low-Maintenance Fade

The sharp, just-left-the-chair fade is giving way to something that holds its shape longer between visits — enough length on top to style differently each day.

It's less about one dramatic cut and more about a shape that still looks intentional on day twelve, not just day one. Ask for a fade with room to grow into, not one that only works fresh.

— Azhan, Hair Stylist
