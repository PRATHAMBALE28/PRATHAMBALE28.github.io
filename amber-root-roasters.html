OUR STORY

Started with one bag of green beans and a home roaster.

Amber Root began in 2019 above a garage in Kalyani Nagar, roasting 2kg at a time on a machine built from spare parts. Three years and a lot of burnt batches later, we moved into the Koregaon Park space — but the rule hasn't changed: we still cup every new lot ourselves before it goes anywhere near a menu.

Today we work directly with three family-run farms in Chikmagalur and Coorg, paying above market rate for lots we'd actually want to drink. No importers, no unnamed "single-origin blends."

— Kabir Anand, Founder & Head Roaster

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

Do you roast on-site?+

Yes — our roaster sits right behind the counter. We roast in small batches three mornings a week, and you're welcome to watch.

Is there parking nearby?+

Street parking is available on Lane 7, and there's a paid lot two minutes' walk from the door.

Can I buy beans to take home?+

Absolutely. Ask at the counter for our current bean flight — we bag 100g and 250g portions fresh, same day.

Do you host private events?+

We open the space for small cuppings and private tastings after hours — reach out through the contact form below.
