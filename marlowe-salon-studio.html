GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

How far in advance should I book?+

We recommend 4–5 days for weekday slots and at least 10 days for weekend appointments — we keep chairs limited on purpose.

Do you offer consultations before a colour service?+

Yes, every colour appointment opens with a 15-minute consultation, included at no extra charge.

Is the studio child-friendly?+

We're a quiet, adults-focused space, but we're happy to recommend nearby sitters for longer appointments.

What's your cancellation policy?+

We ask for 24 hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone on our waitlist.
